# Motivation

Vastior started from a simple frustration: I wanted to see farther in Grim Dawn.

The default camera works fine most of the time, but it can feel too close during normal gameplay. Enemies often become visible only when they are already close enough to aggro, which can make the world feel cramped and reduce the time a player has to read the battlefield.

Vastior was created to make that experience more comfortable.

The goal is not to change the balance of Grim Dawn, automate gameplay, edit characters, modify saves, or give the player a full map-wide view. The goal is much smaller: provide a modest camera improvement that lets the player see a little farther ahead while still keeping the game feeling like Grim Dawn.

## Why This Exists

Vastior exists because:

* the default camera can feel too zoomed in;
* monsters can enter aggro range before the player has enough visual awareness;
* a slightly wider view makes movement, positioning, and exploration feel better;
* the setup should be easier to install, repair, and remove;
* the tool should be safe, reversible, and transparent about what it changes.

## Design Goals

Vastior is built around a few simple goals:

* make the camera feel more comfortable;
* keep the change modest and practical;
* avoid touching character saves, shared stash, or Steam Cloud saves;
* make installation and removal straightforward;
* keep the tool focused on camera-related behavior;
* avoid unnecessary system changes;
* keep the project readable, maintainable, and open-source friendly.

## What Vastior Is Not

Vastior is not a character editor, stash editor, trainer, automation tool, or gameplay overhaul.

It does not exist to bypass progression, change items, modify character data, or interfere with unrelated game systems. It is intended to be a focused camera utility for players who prefer a slightly better view of the battlefield.

## Philosophy

A small quality-of-life tool should stay small.

Vastior should be easy to understand, easy to remove, and safe to use. Every part of the project should be built with that in mind.
